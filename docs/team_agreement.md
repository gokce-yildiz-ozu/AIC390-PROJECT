# Team agreement (W1.1)

Team: Deadline Radar · AIC 390 Prompt Engineering, Özyeğin University, Fall 2026 · Agreed: 2026-11-16

## 1. Roles

| Role | Member | Owns (scoreboard) |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation and safety lead | Serap Gökçe Yıldız | Accuracy with 95% interval, injection success, pass^k |
| Data and retrieval lead | Mert Bozma | Hit rate at k, MRR, unsupported-claim rate |
| Agent and tools lead | Enes Kemal Köylü | Task success, steps per task, cost per task |

- A lead owns the quality of their area, not all the work in it. The lead reviews changes in their area and
  decides there; the other two help with the work.
- Everyone writes code. Each member picks a second area by week 3 and records it in the area coverage table in
  `docs/STATUS.md`, so that everyone has commits in at least two areas by week 7 (R2).

## 2. Presenter rotation

| Week | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Presenter | Gökçe | Enes | Mert | Gökçe | Enes | Mert | Gökçe |

- Everyone presents at least twice. Two members can swap a week if both agree.
- The instructor may ask any of us about any part of the system, so all three read the report before class.

## 3. Meetings and internal deadlines

- **Wednesday, after class:** plan the week, using the instructor's feedback.
- **Monday evening, online:** check the report and the scoreboard together.
- **Internal deadline:** each member's part is pushed by Monday 23:59.
- **Submission:** the report is tagged and submitted by Tuesday 10:00. The course deadline is Tuesday 12:30,
  24 hours before class (R4).

## 4. How we decide

- The lead of an area decides questions in that area.
- If the other two members disagree together, the majority decides.
- A decision counts only once it is written in `docs/decisions.md` (D-001).

## 5. If someone doesn't deliver

"Doesn't deliver" means missing an agreed internal deadline without telling the team beforehand.

1. **First time:** we talk within 24 hours and agree on a new date.
2. **Second time:** the task moves to another member, and this is written in the contributions section of the
   weekly report.
3. **Third time:** we tell the instructor. We raise problems early and do not wait until week 7.

## 6. After the course

- The three of us own the project equally.
- A member who leaves keeps the right to show their own contribution, for example in a portfolio. The name and the
  repository stay with the members who continue.
- Big decisions (license, selling the product, making the repository private) need all three to agree.
