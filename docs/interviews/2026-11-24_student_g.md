# Customer interview: second-year industrial engineering student

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-11-24 |
| Who | Second-year industrial engineering student, taking a statistics course this term. No name or contact kept (R10) |
| Interviewer | Serap Gökçe Yıldız |
| How | WhatsApp messages, in Turkish, sent in two parts; answers translated and shortened here |

The product was described in one sentence after question 2 ("an assistant that reads course announcements and documents, extracts the deadlines and reminds you"); questions 1 and 2 were answered before that.

## Answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How do you track deadlines today? | The syllabus is on the LMS at the start of term, but changes mostly come through WhatsApp groups: if nobody writes "the quiz tomorrow is postponed", they do not hear about it. Very important items go into phone notes or an alarm; the rest they try to keep in their head, "but I can't really" |
| The last deadline you missed or left to the end? | Two weeks ago, in statistics. The instructor wrote the homework on the last page of a weekly slide deck, not in the general announcements, and they opened the slides only in exam week. They found out at about 21:00 on the due date, when someone in the group asked whether to submit a PDF. They submitted by midnight with half of it empty and expect to have lost about half of the points |
| Would you pay? How much? Who should pay? | Would not pay much alone, "we already pay subscriptions for everything". At most 500 TL a month, and only if it saves them in midterm and final weeks. The school should buy it and integrate it into the LMS, "since their own system is messy" |
| What worries you about an AI doing this? | Most of all a wrong date or a missed item: relying on it, they would stop checking themselves, so one miss could mean failing a course. Giving it full access to their school email: emails with instructors or personal information could leak |
| If it gave a wrong date once? | Would probably delete it at once, with no trust left, because tracking for them is its only job. If it can be wrong, they would check the LMS and the slides themselves anyway, so there would be no point using it |

## Limits

- By message, so no follow-up questions were asked.
