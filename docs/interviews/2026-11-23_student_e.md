# Customer interview: first-year student

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-11-23 |
| Who | First-year business student, 6 courses this term. No name or contact kept (R10) |
| Interviewer | Enes Kemal Köylü |
| How | In person, about 10 questions, in Turkish; answers typed during the talk, translated and shortened here |

The product was described in one sentence before question 6; questions 1 to 5 were asked before that.

## Answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How do you track deadlines today? | Friends remind them on WhatsApp, "because I sometimes forget". Dates also come from the LMS and email; what is said in class is usually emailed too; project dates are on the LMS upload page |
| Have you missed one? | Group projects are left to the last evening and friends remind them. Individual homework is sometimes left to the last minute or missed |
| The last one you missed? | An individual homework. The deadline was far away when it was posted, so it was left to the end. Cost about 3% of the grade, "not sure" |
| A moved or unclear date? | Not so far |
| Would you use it? What must it do? | Yes, but "how will it warn me?" It should know whether the work is done and not keep reminding about finished homework |
| When and how often to remind? | Once two weeks before; then daily messages, named as from three days before or through the last week; not only on the last day. Reminders stop when they reply "I did it", or the system sees it by itself. Asked whether this would be a WhatsApp conversation |
| Would you pay? Who should pay? | Perhaps 100–200 TL (asked as a monthly price). Would prefer that the school pays and the tool reads the LMS, so that there is nothing to do |
| What worries you about an AI reading your email? | Private information could leak; it could send emails to instructors in their name |
| If it gave a wrong date once? | "Without a source, that would break my trust"; would probably stop using it. Seeing the source would be better |

## Limits

- One person, a friend of the interviewer, so it is not a sample of students.
- The last question offered the idea of showing the source, so the preference for sources is a prompted answer.
- The price was a guess, with no product to look at.
