# ECON-101 Principles of Economics, Late-Work Policy, Fall 2026

## Late work

ECON-101 does not accept late work. Assignments and reports must be uploaded to the LMS by their deadline; a submission after the deadline receives 0.
This course policy applies under Article 14 of the regulation, which leaves the late-work rule to the course instructor.
