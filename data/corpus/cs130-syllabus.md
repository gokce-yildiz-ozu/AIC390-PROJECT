# CS-130 Introduction to Programming, Syllabus, Fall 2026

## Course information

Instructor: Dr. Kemal Arslan. Lectures on Monday and Thursday, 09:40–11:30. Weekly lab sessions on Wednesday, 13:40–15:30.
The course introduces programming in Python: variables, control flow, functions, lists, dictionaries and files.

## Grading

Weekly lab exercises 20%, programming assignments 30%, midterm exam 20%, final exam 30%.

## Schedule

Programming assignments are announced on the LMS, each with its own due date.

## Late policy

Late programming assignments lose 10% of their grade for each day they are late, for at most 2 days. An assignment more than 2 days late receives 0.
