# Undergraduate Education Regulation

## Article 12

Make-up exams. A student who misses a midterm exam may take the make-up exam only with a documented excuse, such as a medical report. The document must be handed in to the student affairs office within 3 working days after the missed exam. Make-up exams are held in the make-up week of the academic calendar.
